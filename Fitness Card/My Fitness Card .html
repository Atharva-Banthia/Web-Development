Weekly Fitness Routine

Balanced strength, cardio, and recovery schedule

Monday: Upper Body

Focus: Chest, Back & Shoulders

  • Push-ups: 3 sets of 12
  • Dumbbell Rows: 3 sets of 10
  • Shoulder Press: 3 sets of 10

Tuesday: Lower Body

Focus: Legs & Glutes

  • Bodyweight Squats: 5 sets of 10
  • Lunges: 3 sets of 12
  • Planks: 3 sets of 1 minute

Wednesday: Cardio

Focus: Heart Health

  • Running: 30 minutes
  • Jump Rope: 3 sets of 5 minutes
  • High Knees: 3 sets of 1 minute

Thursday: Core

Focus: Abs & Stability

  • Crunches: 3 sets of 15
  • Russian Twists: 3 sets of 20
  • Leg Raises: 3 sets of 12

Friday: Full Body

Focus: Total Body Strength

  • Burpees: 3 sets of 10
  • Kettlebell Swings: 3 sets of 15
  • Mountain Climbers: 3 sets of 1 minute

Saturday: Active Recovery

Focus: Stretching & Mobility

  • Yoga: 30 minutes
  • Foam Rolling: 15 minutes
  • Light Walk: 20 minutes

Sunday: Rest Day

Focus: Recovery & Relaxation

  • Rest and recharge for the week ahead!
